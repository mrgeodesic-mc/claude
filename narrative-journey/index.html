Welcome to Your Leadership Journey

Adaptive Leadership begins with noticing, and one of the most powerful things you can notice is the story you're telling about your own work.

You are the author of your narrative. The stories you choose to tell (internally and externally) shape how colleagues see you, how opportunities come your way, and the direction of your career.

Remember: You are in control of your own narrative. You decide how you develop, how you share your growth, and how you signal your readiness for what comes next.

"What language, image, or principle from Adaptive Leadership has helped you see your own story differently—or tell it more clearly?"
Remember: You can't always control your positional authority—but you can always control your narrative. And your narrative influences how people see your capacity, your readiness, and your trajectory.

Theme 3: Narrative as Career Pathway

Purpose: Help you see storytelling as strategy—not self-promotion—and central to career progression.

Reflect on These Questions:

  • What part of your story do you think opens doors—or could, if told more clearly?
  • What have you learned about yourself through experiments or challenges that signals where you want to go next?
  • How has articulating your growth helped you feel more ownership over your career direction?
  • If your supervisor asked, "What do you want to be known for?" what would you say?
